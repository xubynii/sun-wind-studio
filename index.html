Time period

Sun

How the massing shades the ground, plazas and neighbouring buildings, how much sunlight it takes from them, and how much direct sun each of its own faces receives. Shadows in the viewport follow the date and hour. Vancouver checks new shadow on parks and plazas at the equinox between 10:00 and 16:00.

Wind

Pedestrian-level flow around the site with and without the tower, downwash at the windward base, and the wind the tower meets with height. One wind case, or the time period as eight sectors of the seasonal Vancouver rose. Indicative, not a CFD result.

Rain

How the massing meets Vancouver's rain: which faces take the driving rain and how much, the rain shadow the building casts on the ground, how much water the roofs and terraces collect and shed, and, when the site model carries a terrain surface, where run-off pools on the ground after a storm.Driving rain on each face against the exposure bands architects use, the ground and entrances the building, podium and canopy keep dry, the glass each façade zone's overhangs and balconies shelter, the roof catchment, drainage and harvesting, and where run-off pools on the site's topography and whether it reaches the tower's base.

Design storm

Rainfall and drainage

Driving rain is read in the manner of ISO 15927-3 and BS 8104: an annual index per face from the rain rose, with wall factors that rise toward the top of a tall wall. The rain shadow is rendered from the storm angle, wind speed over drop fall speed. Catchment and run-off come from a flow-routing model of the terraces and roof; the viewport shows the flow paths while the Rain overlay is on.

Massing efficiency

Energy efficiency read from the form alone, before any envelope or system is chosen: form factor and surface-to-volume ratio, a heat-loss index at typical construction, compactness against a square prism of the same height and volume, façade orientation balance, daylit floor area, and exposed roofs and soffits. The review on the right updates live as you shape the massing.

How to read it

Ratings A to E are bands for supertall towers. A compact shaft rates A on form factor at 0.30 m² of envelope per m² of floor; articulated towers with voids and terraces drift toward D and E, and the heat-loss ratio says what that costs against the equivalent square prism. Daylit area counts floor within 6 m of a façade or void. Design development carries these into the full energy model with the façade zones, construction and systems.

Energy

A monthly energy balance per program zone in the manner of ISO 13790: envelope losses by orientation from the carved floors, ventilation with heat recovery, internal gains on program schedules, and solar gains through the window-to-wall ratio, SHGC and shading devices of each façade zone. Results are read against Vancouver's rezoning targets for TEUI, TEDI and GHGI and update live as you edit.

Systems and loads

Programs are derived from the massing by plate size and height (office, housing, hotel, retail, public, plant). Window-to-wall ratio, SHGC and device transmission come from the Façade tab; the device transmission uses the last sun run where there is one.

Façade

Design the façade by zone: a face over a range of levels, with window size, glass, an external shading device, PV and operable openings. The model and the floor plan in the review redraw as you edit; the solar heat table re-reads the last sun run instantly.

Daylight

Read against LEED v4.1 EQ Daylight: spatial daylight autonomy sDA300/50% at 40, 55 and 75% of floor for 1, 2 and 3 points, with annual sunlight exposure ASE1000/250h held at or under 10%. Here sDA is a daylight-factor proxy and ASE a sun sweep; the solar heat and glare of each zone come from the same run.

About these studies

Indicative early-design studies, not compliance analyses: shadows are rendered hour by hour on the site model, the wind is a two-dimensional pedestrian-level flow, and the daylight and ventilation readings use simplified rules in the manner of the standards they cite. An uploaded massing is sliced into floors and carved as a 1 m solid/void grid; in Design development the file can also be rendered as modeled.
